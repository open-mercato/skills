# Quality gate (step 6)

Apply before writing the report. Score each item 0 (absent or contradicted), 1 (present but vague or partial), 2 (concrete and supported).

1. Every persona line carries a tag and a source that exists; assumed lines are listed under *No basis for*.
2. No name, age, city, photo, or biography anywhere.
3. Every persona comes from a segment the brief's Target group names or the data shows.
4. Every finding carries `[SYNTHETIC]` on its own line and names the persona ids behind it.
5. Every finding is paired with a real-user check: who to recruit (role), what to ask or measure, and which brief assumption (`A0n`) it settles when one applies.
6. The report uses "would", never "validated", "confirmed", "users said", or "users want".
7. Under `adversary`, no walkthrough that merely agrees survives; under any stance, a zero-barrier result explains why with the screens.
8. Screens judged from a prototype or app have 📸 evidence; narrative walks say "narrative" and never describe a screen the document does not.
9. Nothing in the report edits or pretends to edit the brief, a spec, or a tracker item.

Critical gates: items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8. A zero means the report is not ready; fix it or drop the finding.

## Slop signals to remove on sight

- A persona that reads like a stock photo caption.
- "Users want" or "customers need" anywhere.
- A finding the material cannot trace to a persona line.
- A walkthrough that praises the flow in every step.
- A quote attributed to a persona as if a person said it.
- A screen described in a narrative walk that neither brief nor spec contains.
