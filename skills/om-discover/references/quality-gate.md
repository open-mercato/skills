# Check the draft

Apply before the skeptic reads the draft and after any corrections, including in quick mode. Keep the checks internal unless the user asks for an audit. A documented unknown is an acceptable result; an unsupported assertion presented as a fact is not.

## Source fidelity

- Each canonical claim has the appropriate source tag and a real pointer, or is an explicit assumption with its origin or lack of material stated. A summary or cross-reference resolves to that canonical claim.
- Sources support the wording and its scope. A team decision does not prove demand, a stated preference does not prove payment, and a repeated citation does not add independent evidence.
- Measurements have provenance, quotes match the notes, and benchmarks have a link and date. Chosen targets are attributed decisions, not invented baselines.
- No unsupported persona details or situations appear. Synthetic claims stay in Hypotheses to test with a real-world check.
- Missing material is not filled with plausible prose. The independent source basis, Coverage arithmetic, separate synthetic count and actual collection-plan entries match the draft.

Correct mistakes in these checks from the material. When the information is unavailable, remove the overstatement or mark the uncertainty and report the correction; do not ask the user to repair your transcription error.

## Decisions and readiness

- Every decision, business rule and non-goal has its stable id, status, owner or `proposal`, review date or explicit trigger (or an explicit unknown), and required path to change. A missing review condition blocks only work that depends on it. An unowned choice required by Now creates a blocking question; an optional later proposal need not block current work.
- Now completes the job claimed for it, or is explicitly a proposed experiment with implementation readiness withheld. Later and Non-goals do not contradict it.
- Every unresolved question says what it blocks and who can answer it. Do not treat a billing, access or essential-flow gap as harmless merely because it can be written down later.
- The mode's relevant readiness requirements are met or state what is missing. An agreement to accept risk does not replace evidence for Problems and Target group.

An unsupported fact, false source, unattributed active decision, or false readiness verdict must be corrected before showing or writing the brief. If a consequential decision remains open, the draft may say so and await confirmation as an incomplete brief.

## Problem, solution and test

- Is the need supported by the recorded situation, with inferred motives or causes kept explicit? Where choosing an approach matters, does the decision explain the relevant alternative considered without reopening settled scope?
- Does Now address the problem within its stated conditions: timing, price, access, trust, effort or other constraints the sources actually name?
- If not, does the summary expose the mismatch and the decision it requires? Do not rewrite the user's need to fit a convenient implementation.
- Does the proposed test measure the assumption at stake? Choose a prototype's form and detail for the specific learning question. Existing data, observation or manual delivery may answer it without a new interface. Distinguish a verbal intention, a commitment, a booking and a payment. Do not call an interview a purchase test.
- Does the test have an observable result and a consequence for the next decision? Unknown thresholds or dates stay unknown; a provisional choice is attributed to its owner.

## Compression and plain language

Read the Decision summary as someone who missed the session. They should be able to identify the decision, basis, scope, consequential unknown and next action without reading the full history. Aim for 500 to 900 words at most, shorter with sparse material; do not pad toward a minimum.

Keep full rules and decisions in their canonical rows. Remove repeated scope descriptions, restated conclusions, method vocabulary and unsupported slogans. Empty optional headings can say why they are deferred in one line. Put history and detailed evidence in their existing sections and sources; preserve ids, source fidelity and actual constraints while shortening prose.
